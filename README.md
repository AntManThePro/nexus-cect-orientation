### What This Teaches

Field techs upload continuously. QA owns the publish decision.

- Complete Blue “before” coverage for every serviced area
- Matching Green “after” coverage
- Red deficiencies clearly documented
- First blue timestamp = job start
- Final green timestamp = job completion (duration history)
- Halfway status once all expected blue media is present
- QA-ready once green + reds are complete

Detailed required shot list included for:
- Hood & Kitchen Interior
- Duct & Access Panels 1–3
- Rooftop & Fan

N/A is used when a component does not exist at the site.
